WW2 BT 0.7.3 Lua16
==================

Built from the WW2 BT 0.7.2 Lua15 archives (lua-0.7.2/delivery) and the native Lua
project in project/. Deliveries: delivery/WW2 BT 0.7.3 Lua16 editable.w3x and
delivery/WW2 BT 0.7.3 Lua16 protected.w3x (identical gameplay data and script).

Changes
-------
MacArthur / SB2C
- SB2C bombs now deal exactly 4000 damage. The bomb's final damage is set in a damage
  event after armor and attack-type modifiers (previously Chaos damage could double it).
- SB2C Precision Strike (AM72) costs 60 mana (was 70). Panel text and AI threshold updated.
- SB2C Group Strike (AM73) shows the 500-radius target circle (Ncl3 options 1 -> 3).
- Group Strike waves strike the 3 enemy ground units with the highest individual Blood
  Point value (Bounty_Value) inside the circle; equal values are chosen at random.
- After every battle (normal rounds, each bonus turn, each final tournament fight), every
  commander building with mana is refilled to full (hooked into Carrier_Stop).

Commander quick-select button
- A button showing the chosen commander's icon sits directly right of the idle-builder
  button. Click selects your commander building; double-click also centres the camera.
  Hidden until a commander is chosen. Selection is local to the clicking player.

Italian Elite Squad (hIE0, 500 gold)
- All four soldier types (hIE1-hIE4): 600 hitpoints (was 350).
- Breda PG rifleman (hIE2): attack cooldown 1.1 (was 0.9). Every attack is a four-round
  burst: the normal shot plus 3 scripted rounds 0.12 s apart, each a visible Bullet.mdx
  projectile flying to the target and dealing the same damage as the first round.
  Army-overview DPS and AI squad values updated (AI HP 3000, ground DPS 201).
